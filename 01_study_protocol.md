# Study Protocol

## Study Title

A Randomized, Parallel-Group Study Evaluating a Lifestyle Intervention on Glycemic Control in Adults With Type 2 Diabetes

## Study Objective

To evaluate the effect of a 12-week lifestyle intervention on glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes.

## Study Design

- Simulated randomized parallel-group clinical trial
- 100 participants
- 12-week study duration
- Adults aged 30–65 years with type 2 diabetes

## Study Visits

- Week 0 — Screening / Baseline
- Week 4 — Follow-up 1
- Week 8 — Follow-up 2
- Week 12 — End of Study

## Primary Outcome

Change in HbA1c from baseline to Week 12.

## Secondary Outcomes

- Body weight
- BMI
- Fasting blood glucose
- Blood pressure
- Medication information
- Treatment adherence
- Adverse events

## Data Quality Objectives

1. Identify missing required clinical data.
2. Detect values outside predefined acceptable ranges.
3. Identify logical and cross-field inconsistencies.
4. Generate, document and manage clinical data queries.
5. Track query resolution and data cleaning.
6. Produce a final clean dataset suitable for analysis.

## Project Scope

This is a simulated educational portfolio project using synthetic data. No real patient information or personally identifiable health information will be used.
